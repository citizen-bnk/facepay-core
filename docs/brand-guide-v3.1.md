# FACEPAY Digital Brand Guide v3.1

21 October 2026 · Implementation addendum to the approved October 2026 v3 guide. The approved master PDF and logo artwork remain unchanged.

Use official artwork only. Do not retype the custom wordmark. Compact logos: minimum 140 px; full logo with tagline: 240 px; mark: 24 px. Preserve clear space equal to the F upper blade height. Use reversed artwork on black and the supplied boxed PAY variation on white.

Poppins is the interface typeface, with Regular body text and Medium to Bold headings. Code uses monospace. Gold gradients are reserved for artwork, large headlines and the gold light motif. Buttons use flat gold with black text. Keep one flowing gold motif per layout.

Brand line: **Face your money.** Campaign: **Pay with a look. Fast. Secure. Convenient.** Short interaction: **Look. Pay. Go.** Use warm South African imagery and plain language. Say iris rather than retina. Format rand amounts as R1 245.00.

Website: black brand opening, official artwork, one gold flow, light information sections. Mobile: black splash, navy surfaces, Poppins, official mark and flat gold actions. Back office: black navigation, light grey canvas, white cards and compact official logo. Developer documentation: the same identity with a navy coding workspace and clear sandbox labels.

Developer membership is US$100 annually. Clerk registration, Neon account storage and Stripe sandbox billing are connected. Real charges require Stripe business activation. FACEPAY payment and biometric providers remain simulated; the visual design does not certify live financial or biometric capabilities.
